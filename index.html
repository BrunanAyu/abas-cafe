About the Café

A warm corner of Dessie

AB-AS Café is a cozy café in Arada, Dessie — a place to sit back, enjoy good coffee and great food, and spend time with friends. With red upholstered seating, warm golden lighting and colorful walls, the space is made for relaxing.

Coffee Burgers Pizza Refreshing Drinks Other Food Cozy Atmosphere

Come up to the 2nd floor, in front of Rukiya Hall — grab a table, order a cup, and enjoy the atmosphere.
